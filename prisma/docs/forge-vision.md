## The one-liner

**The Etsy + Lovable for AIoT hardware** — everyday creators (moms, language
enthusiasts, caregivers) design, prototype and sell physical products that change lives.

**Tagline:** Design life-changing AIoT hardware with AI. Prototype for $200. Sell on the
world's first AI-powered physical products marketplace.

## Vision, mission, north star

**Vision.** A world where anyone with an idea can turn it into a physical, life-changing
product — no engineering degree, no factory connections. Democratise hardware the way AI
democratised software and Etsy democratised crafts.

**Mission.** Empower entrepreneurs, parents and domain experts to build and sell AIoT
products that solve real human problems — intuitive AI design tools, a manufacturing
marketplace, and $200 sample credits.

**North star.** Become the default platform where mission-driven creators launch products
that measurably improve lives: products shipped, lives affected (multilingual toddlers,
babies sleeping more safely, children with affordable prosthetics), creator earnings.

## The problem

AI made *software* buildable by anyone. Hardware is still gated by expertise, prototyping
cost, supply chains and manufacturing access.

The people with the domain knowledge — multilingual moms, parents of disabled children,
caregivers — have the ideas and no route to market. The result is slow innovation exactly
where it matters most: baby safety, assistive tech, language learning, everyday AIoT.

## The solution

Four parts, one platform:

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| **AI Design Studio** | Describe the idea in plain language — *"a clip-on device that reads any physical Russian children's book aloud, with character voices, for my multilingual toddler"*. AI generates and iterates the 3D model (Blender integration), suggests components, checks manufacturability. |
| **$200 prototype credit** | Every new creator gets credit toward real manufactured samples through partner manufacturers. |
| **Marketplace** | Sell digital templates (3D files + build guides) or full physical products, with production and fulfilment facilitated by the platform. |
| **Impact-first curation** | Every listing leads with the human story and the life-changing potential, not the spec sheet. |

### Flagship products (founder-built)

These are the proof, and they're also the content — see the Hardware pillar in the
[Coding Mom guide](./coding-mom.md).

1. **Multilingual physical book reader** for toddlers.
2. **Comfortable, affordable baby breathing tracker** — the better alternative to the
   foot sock.
3. **Modular, low-cost robotic aids** for disabled children.

## Market

Exploding AIoT and digital-manufacturing markets; a large creator economy sitting on top
of the parenting, tech and wellness verticals; and real, underserved demand for accessible
assistive, educational and family products.

## Business model

- **Marketplace commission** — 10–20% on templates and physical products.
- **Premium subscription** — advanced AI features, unlimited designs, priority manufacturing.
- **Manufacturing & fulfilment fees** — margin on facilitated production and shipping.
- **Featured listings / impact sponsorships** — brands and nonprofits promoting high-impact products.
- **Enterprise / white-label** — companies using the platform for internal innovation.

Early revenue comes from the founder's own product sales plus marketplace fees as the
catalogue grows.

## Go to market

Solo founder, and a mom — so growth runs on authentic storytelling rather than spend.

**Phase 1 — personal journey (0–6 months).** Document everything on TikTok, Instagram and
YouTube Shorts: building the book reader as a Vietnamese-English mom, testing the first
AI-designed breathing tracker, talking to parents and manufacturers. Share the failures,
the wins, and the interviews with moms, speech therapists, pediatricians and hardware
people. Build a community of parents and aspiring creators.

> This phase is not a marketing plan sitting next to the work — **it is Coding Mom**, and
> it has already started. That's why nothing gets built off-camera.

**Phase 2 — community & marketplace launch.** Invite early creators with free credits and
hands-on support. Their impact stories become the marketing. SEO around "how to build AI
hardware as a non-engineer", multilingual parenting, baby safety innovation.

**Phase 3 — scale.** Creator programmes; partnerships with parenting influencers, maker
communities and assistive-tech organisations; press around democratising life-changing
hardware.

## Competition

| Who | What they have | What they don't |
|---|---|---|
| **Lovable.dev** | Vibe-based AI creation — the inspiration | Software only |
| **Etsy / maker platforms** | Physical goods, real buyers | No AI design, nothing hardware-specific |
| **Fictiv, Xometry** | Serious manufacturing | Built for professionals, not for non-technical creators |
| **Owlet et al.** | Finished baby-tech products | Closed — one company's roadmap, not a platform |

**The differentiation is being end-to-end** — AI design *plus* sample credit *plus*
marketplace *plus* an impact lens — told by a founder who is her own first customer.

## Founder

A multilingual mom building tools for real family needs. Domain expertise in languages and
motherhood, committed to transparent, impact-driven building.
