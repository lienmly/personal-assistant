# Resources

Links worth keeping for this project. They touch three different parts of the
board — the Art track, the Next Fest track, and the design feedback that keeps
generating Build rows.

> **A link here is a pointer, not a decision.** Nothing on this page has been
> tried or committed to. It is the pile you come back to when the job comes up,
> and where a tool has not actually been looked at this page says so rather than
> describing it confidently.

## Art and asset generation

### Hunyuan3D — WorldClaw

[Project page and results reel](https://tencent-hunyuan.github.io/Hunyuan3D-WorldClaw/#results) · Tencent Hunyuan

Billed on its own page as *agentic 3D open-world generation at scale*. That page
is a results reel rather than a product, so the things that would decide whether
it is usable here — what it costs, what licence the output carries, whether the
model is downloadable or hosted — are all unknown from the link.

Where it touches the board: the **Art** track. That work runs on somebody else's
schedule, which is the whole reason Art is a track of its own, so this is a link
to show him rather than a plan to act on.

### Grok Imagine — game props / UI kit template

[The template](https://grok.com/imagine/template/game-props-ui-kit) · needs a signed-in Grok account

A generation template for game prop and UI-kit art. It could not be read without
signing in, so what it actually produces is unverified and the sentence above is
the template's own name and nothing more.

Where it *might* touch the board: the resource-UI work. Hearts earned and hearts
spent on comfort currently look like the same thing on screen and are not — see
the design feedback page — and that is a readability job with real art in it. A
look before that pass, not a commitment to generated assets.

## Steam Next Fest

[The official hub](https://store.steampowered.com/sale/nextfest)

The page to re-check, because it is where Valve announces the edition. Read on
**15 August 2026**, its title says "Steam Next Fest: October 2026 Edition — Coming
Soon", which confirms the edition this whole plan is aimed at and **still does not
give the week**.

That gap is the single most load-bearing unknown on the project. Every date on the
Next Fest track is derived from a guessed week — Mon 12 to Mon 19, following the
2024 and 2025 pattern — and confirming it in Steamworks is the first task in the
track. This page is the second place to watch for it.
